# What are micro-exercises?

> Low-intensity movements that take 30–60 seconds each, embedded into work gaps — no changing clothes, no time block, no gym.

## Definition

Micro-exercises (a.k.a. "exercise snacks") are short bursts of physical activity slipped into daily life: 15 heel raises while waiting for a build, a 5-second shoulder-blade squeeze before a meeting, one round of belly breathing before replying to a message. The goal is not intensity or duration — it is **the frequency with which you break up sitting**.

## Why it works (what research says)

| Finding | Source |
|---------|--------|
| Five minutes of light walking every 30 minutes of sitting reduced post-meal blood sugar spikes by **58%** and blood pressure by 4–5 mmHg — the only tested dose that significantly improved both | [Columbia University (Diaz team) randomized crossover trial, 2023](https://www.cuimc.columbia.edu/news/rx-prolonged-sitting-five-minute-stroll-every-half-hour) · [Paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36728338/) |
| Vigorous intermittent lifestyle physical activity (VILPA — e.g., hurrying for a train, climbing stairs) at a median of just **4.4 min/day** was associated with **26–30% lower** all-cause/cancer mortality and **32–34% lower** cardiovascular mortality (UK Biobank, ~25k participants, wrist accelerometers) | [Nature Medicine, Stamatakis et al. (2022/2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-022-02100-x) |

Mechanism in brief: when you sit, the muscle pump idles and glucose/lipid metabolism depends on intermittent contraction to "restart"; brief activity acutely improves insulin sensitivity and circulation. Micro-exercises reduce "exercise" from a willpower-heavy block task into decision-free habit fragments.

## Who it's for

- **Sedentary knowledge workers**: engineers, designers, writers — 8+ hours a day in front of screens
- **People with no time to work out**: no gym membership, no gear, but want to offset the harm of sitting
- **People who forget to take breaks**: three hours vanish once you enter flow (exactly what the desk pet reminds you about)
- **Meeting-heavy schedules**: moves you can do invisibly between meetings (under-desk heel raises, belly breathing)

Not for: systematic muscle-building or fat-loss goals — micro-exercises offset sitting; they don't replace training.

## The 7 built-in exercises in micro-pet

| Exercise | How | Duration |
|----------|-----|----------|
| 🦶 Heel Raises (under desk) | Lift heels up & down ×15 | 45s |
| 🪑 Chair Micro-Squat | Lift hips off chair 3s ×5 | 40s |
| 💪 Shoulder Blade Squeeze | Squeeze blades 5s ×5 | 35s |
| 🧘 Neck Stretch | Tilt head sideways 15s each | 30s |
| 🦵 Seated Leg Hold | Legs lifted off floor 10s ×3 | 40s |
| ✋ Wrist Stretch | Palm-out press 15s | 30s |
| 🌬️ Belly Breathing | Inhale 4, exhale 6 ×5 | 60s |

All doable **while seated, invisible during meetings**. Fully customizable via [`~/.micro-pet/exercises.json`](../README.en.md#configuration-micro-pet).
